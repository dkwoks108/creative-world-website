# Creative World — Content Architecture & Editorial Policy

## 1. Brand Positioning Statement
Creative World is a Jaipur-focused digital growth agency that unifies performance marketing, local SEO, social brand content, and web conversion infrastructure into a connected acquisition engine.

---

## 2. Content Pillars & Messaging Tone
- **Human & Outcome-Driven**: Plain-English explanations of digital marketing without confusing buzzwords.
- **Jaipur Market Context**: Direct references to Jaipur economic hubs (C-Scheme, Malviya Nagar, Vaishali Nagar, Tonk Road).
- **Commercial Transparency**: Clear INR pricing tiers (Starter ₹7,999–₹12,999, Growth ₹15,000–₹25,000, Premium ₹40,000+) and direct ad account billing.

---

## 3. Strict "No Fake Proof" Editorial Policy
- **Client Logos**: Placeholder logos are grouped under generic Jaipur sector names (`Jaipur Education Client`, `Jaipur Real Estate Client`, `Jaipur Retail Client`) until owner-provided SVG vector logos are uploaded.
- **Case Studies**: All work showcases are explicitly titled as **Growth Playbooks** / **Industry Growth Frameworks** until client signed permissions and verified metrics are provided.
- **Client Testimonials**: Clearly labeled as industry sentiment benchmarks until signed client quotes are received.
- **No Artificial Claims**: Zero fabricated ROAS figures or fake claims of guaranteed #1 Google rankings.
