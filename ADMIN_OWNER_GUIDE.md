## 2. Managing Blog Articles & Growth Insights

### A. Creating a New Article
1. Navigate to **Blog CMS** -> click **Create Article**.
2. Fill in the **Article Title** (e.g., *Local SEO Strategies for Jaipur Retailers*).
3. The **URL Slug** will automatically generate.
4. Write your article summary in **Article Excerpt**.
5. Write your article text in **Article Body Content**. Use the **Insert Internal Link** button to easily link to agency services, playbooks, or growth audit pages.
6. Select **Category** and **Author**.
7. Configure **SEO & Keywords**: Set primary keyword, SEO title tag, meta description, and `noindex`/`nofollow` flags if needed.
8. Preview how your article will look on search engines using the **Google SERP Snippet Preview** and **Social Sharing OpenGraph Card Preview**.
9. Add **Article FAQs** for structured Schema.org search rich results.
10. Set **Status** (**Draft**, **Review**, **Scheduled**, or **Published**) and click **Save Article**.

### B. Autosave & Revision Restoration
- **Live Autosave**: As you type, the status indicator will transition from `● Unsaved Changes` to `Saving...` and `✓ Saved`.
- **Revision History**: Historical snapshots are saved automatically. Click the **Restore** button next to any revision snapshot to revert content to a previous state.

### C. Bulk Actions & Management
- Navigate to **Blog CMS** dashboard (`/cw-control-x7k9m2/posts`).
- Use status tabs (**ALL**, **PUBLISHED**, **DRAFT**, **REVIEW**, **SCHEDULED**, **ARCHIVED**) to filter posts.
- Use checkboxes to select multiple articles for **Bulk Publish**, **Bulk Draft**, **Bulk Archive**, or **Bulk Delete** (with safety confirmation dialogs).
- Click the copy icon to **Duplicate** any post into a new Draft with one click.

## 3. Taxonomies, Tags & Author Profiles

### A. Category Taxonomy (`/cw-control-x7k9m2/categories`)
- Create, edit, and describe article categories (e.g., *Performance Marketing*, *Local SEO*).
- Categories with assigned posts cannot be accidentally deleted until posts are reassigned.

### B. Tag Taxonomy (`/cw-control-x7k9m2/tags`)
- Create and edit keywords/tags to organize editorial articles across search indexes.

### C. Author Profiles (`/cw-control-x7k9m2/authors`)
- Create and manage author profiles with custom names, roles (e.g., *Head of Growth*), bios, and avatar URLs.

## 4. Managing Growth Audit Inquiries & Leads

1. Navigate to **Inquiries** from the left sidebar.
2. View incoming lead submissions from your Growth Audit form and Contact page.
3. Click any client name to open the detailed profile showing Name, Email, Phone, Business Name, Website URL, Growth Goal, and Project Budget.
4. Update client status (**New** -> **Contacted** -> **Qualified** -> **Closed**).
5. Use **Private Internal Notes** to log call summary notes, proposal details, or follow-up dates.

## 5. Media Library & Asset Uploads

1. Navigate to **Media Library**.
2. Click **Upload Image** to upload WebP, PNG, or JPEG graphics (up to 5MB).
3. Click **Copy URL** to copy the image URL for use in blog posts or landing pages.
4. Add descriptive **Alt Text** and **Captions** for maximum Google Image SEO rankings.

## 6. Security, Session Management & Password Settings

### A. Session Policy & Automatic Inactivity Warning
- **Idle Timeout**: If inactive for 30 minutes, your session will expire automatically to protect agency data.
- **Inactivity Warning**: An alert banner will appear 5 minutes prior to timeout. Click **Continue Session** to extend your active work session.
- **Absolute Limit**: Regardless of activity, sessions expire after 12 hours.

### B. Password Management & Active Sessions
- Navigate to **Settings & Audit** (`/cw-control-x7k9m2/settings`).
- **Change Password**: Change your admin password anytime (minimum 12 characters). Changing your password automatically logs out all other devices for security.
- **Active Sessions Control**: View all active browsers and devices logged into your account. Click **Revoke** on any device or **Log Out Other Devices** to instantly end unauthorized sessions.
- **Audit Logs**: Inspect historical security logs including logins, password changes, and blog updates.
