# Creative World — AI Image & Visual Asset Generation Briefs

## 1. Visual Style Guidelines
All generated visual assets for Creative World must strictly adhere to the dark editorial visual direction:
- **Palette**: Deep Obsidian (`#0A0B0E`), Graphite Surface (`#12141A`), Electric Signal Cyan (`#00F0FF`), Muted Grey (`#8A8F9E`).
- **Aesthetic**: Premium, minimalist, high contrast, subtle glowing grid lines, 3D geometric abstract shapes. Zero tacky stock photos.
