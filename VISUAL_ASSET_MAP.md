# Creative World — Visual Asset Inventory & Production Map

## 1. Asset Strategy & Selection Methodology
Rather than cluttering every route with generic stock photos, Creative World utilizes a highly curated visual library of **19 strategic assets** across 3 priority tiers.

- **P0 (Essential Brand & Industry Visuals)**: 7 high-impact commercial & sector imagery defining agency positioning and industry relevance.
- **P1 (High-Value Service & Playbook Storytelling)**: 9 editorial graphics and growth playbook covers.
- **P2 (Supporting Editorial Insight Covers)**: 3 article covers for the Insights knowledge hub.
- **P3 (Decorative / Gratuitous Images)**: 0 (Rejected to protect page speed, conversion clarity, and UI elegance).
