# Creative World — Image Style System Specification

## 1. Photography Style & Aesthetic Direction
Creative World imagery adheres to a **Commercial Dark Editorial Aesthetic**. Images must feel cinematic, authentic, and deliberate—communicating business strategy, technical execution, and commercial ambition.

- **Aesthetic Benchmark**: Architectural Digest meets Commercial Advertising Editorial.
- **Tonal Identity**: Controlled dark surfaces, deep shadows, crisp specular highlights, natural skin tones, and rich architectural textures (sandstone, charcoal, brushed metal, glass).
- **Core Mood**: Strategic, focused, authoritative, modern Indian entrepreneurship.

---

## 2. Human Casting & Authenticity Directives
When people are featured in Creative World imagery, they must strictly represent real, credible business founders and operators.

- **Demographics**: Believable Indian entrepreneurs, growth strategists, marketers, and business owners aged 28–50.
- **Expressions & Poses**: Focused, thoughtful, natural posture, engaged in real work (evaluating strategy, reviewing analytics, overseeing production).
- **Wardrobe**: Contemporary professional attire (smart jackets, tailored linen shirts, clean architectural wardrobe).
- **Skin & Anatomy**: 100% natural skin texture, realistic pores, authentic hand posture, zero plastic AI smoothness.
- **STRICTLY BANNED**:
  - ❌ Exaggerated corporate smiles or thumbs-up poses.
  - ❌ Fake boardroom handshakes or "pointing at a laptop" group shots.
  - ❌ Stereotypical stock call-center imagery or artificial models.

---

## 3. Environmental Setting & Jaipur Influence
Jaipur's rich architectural heritage and commercial vitality influence the visual environment without relying on tourism clichés.

- **Contemporary Jaipur Context**: Modern commercial offices, contemporary cafes, high-end jewelry design workshops, textile studios, and sleek residential developments.
- **Architectural Elements**: Warm pink sandstone textures, geometric lattice shadows (jaali-inspired light patterns), minimalist concrete, dark slate floors, and floor-to-ceiling glass.
- **STRICTLY BANNED**:
  - ❌ Tourist clichés (elephants, camels, tourist crowds, Fort palaces, snake charmers).
  - ❌ Generic Dubai or New York skyscraper skylines that lack local geographic plausibility.

---

## 4. Lighting & Lens Language
- **Camera Perspective**: 35mm (contextual environments), 50mm (authentic human portraits), and 85mm (macro product/craft details).
- **Depth of Field**: Controlled depth of field (f/2.8 to f/5.6). Subtle background blur that maintains environmental context.
- **Lighting**: Single directional key light (emulating morning/late afternoon sunlight through architectural windows) paired with soft dark shadows.
- **Color Temperature**: Dual temperature lighting—warm architectural ambient light (2800K–3200K) contrasted with cool studio accent fill (5600K–6500K).

---

## 5. Web Composition & Text-Safe Zones
All visual assets are designed specifically for responsive web layouts.

- **Aspect Ratios**:
  - Desktop: `16:9` widescreen or `3:2` editorial ratio.
  - Mobile: `4:3` or `4:5` vertical ratio.
- **Negative Space**: Minimum 40% empty/dark negative space on either the left or right third of the frame to accommodate overlaid typography and UI controls.
- **Focal Point Placement**: Rule-of-thirds alignment with clear separation between subject and dark background.

---

## 6. Brand Color Integration
Generated photography must naturally complement Creative World's dark UI tokens:

- **Obsidian Background**: `#07090E`
- **Surface Primary**: `#0F131C`
- **Text Primary**: `#F1F5F9`
- **Signal Cyan Accent**: `#00F0FF` (Introduced as subtle ambient light streaks, screen highlights, or specular reflections—NEVER saturated neon filters).
- **Quantum Violet**: `#7C3AED` (Subtle shadow color grade fill).

---

## 7. Forbidden Styles & Anti-Patterns Checklist
- ❌ **No Tacky Stock Photos**: Avoid generic stock library aesthetic.
- ❌ **No AI Plasticity**: Avoid overly smooth, glossy, or doll-like AI human renders.
- ❌ **No Cyberpunk Overkill**: Avoid glowing neon eyes, futuristic holograms, or sci-fi UI overlays floating in mid-air.
- ❌ **No Fake Interfaces**: Avoid generating fake Google Search or Facebook Ads software screenshots.
- ❌ **No Text or Logos in Image**: Zero AI-generated gibberish text, fake brand signs, or watermarks.
